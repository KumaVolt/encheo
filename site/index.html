One player for everything you already have: your Plex, Jellyfin and Emby servers and the files on your network. It gathers them into one library with artwork and progress that follows you between devices, and plays every file as it is, from lossless music to 4K HDR.

Everything in one home

Continue Watching and recently added from every source, side by side. Progress and watched marks go back to your server, so you can pick up on another device.

Find something to watch

Sort films and shows by title, date added, release date or rating. Filter by genre, country, decade, age rating or what you haven't seen yet, and jump through the alphabet with the A–Z rail.

Plays the file as it is

Playback runs on mpv, so 4K HDR, lossless audio and unusual formats play without your server converting anything. Skip intros and credits, find subtitles through your server, and go straight on to the next episode.

Music, with the words

Artists, albums, playlists and shuffle, with synced lyrics that light up line by line. On Android it keeps playing with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen.

From the desk to the sofa

The same app on your computer, phone, tablet and Google TV. Use a mouse, touch, a keyboard or the TV remote.

Download

Encheo is early software: every release so far is a preview. macOS and Android get the most testing.

  • macOSApple silicon

    Needs mpv: brew install mpv. Not notarised yet, so right-click the app and choose Open the first time.

  • WindowsInstaller, 10 and 11

    Not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn: choose More info, then Run anyway. There's also a portable zip.

  • Linuxx86-64

    Needs libmpv: sudo apt install libmpv2, or your distribution's package.

  • Android and Google TVComing to Google Play

    Encheo for phones, tablets and TVs will be free on Google Play, with automatic updates. Until then you can build it yourself from the source.

  • iPhone and iPadExperimental, iOS 16 or later

    Unsigned: install it with AltStore or SideStore, which sign it with your Apple ID.

Older versions and release notes are on GitHub.

Questions

Do I need a media server?

No. Encheo also plays straight from folders on your computer and from shares on your network (SMB, NFS, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP and DLNA). A server adds things like progress that follows you between devices.

Is there an account, or tracking?

There's no Encheo account and no analytics. Encheo talks to your own servers, and to a few public services for extras such as lyrics and trailers. The privacy policy lists them.

Does my server need to convert videos?

No. Encheo plays the original file, so your server doesn't have to transcode and you get the full picture and sound quality.

Is Encheo made by Plex, Jellyfin or Emby?

No. Encheo is an independent, open-source app by KumaVolt that works with those servers. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by them.
