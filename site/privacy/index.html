Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Encheo is a media player made by KumaVolt. This page covers the Encheo app on every platform, including the version on Google Play, and this website.

In short: there's no Encheo account, no analytics and no advertising. KumaVolt doesn't receive any data from the app, apart from the order details of a support purchase if you make one. The app talks to the servers you add and, for a few extras, to the public services listed below.

What the app stores on your device

  • The servers and shares you add, and what you need to sign in to them (for Plex, an access token from Plex; for other sources, the user name and password you enter).
  • Your settings, favourites, playback positions and preferred audio and subtitle languages.

This stays in the app's own folder on your device and is never sent to KumaVolt. Removing a source in Settings, or signing out of Plex, deletes its sign-in; uninstalling the app deletes everything. Artwork is only kept in memory while the app runs.

Who the app talks to

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Your own servers and shares (Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, SMB, NFS, WebDAV, SFTP, FTP, DLNA)Whenever you browse or playThe requests needed to list and play your media, with your sign-in for that server. Plex and Jellyfin also receive your watch progress, as with any player.
Plex (plex.tv)Only if you sign in with PlexSign-in, your list of servers and Home profiles, your watchlist, and searches in Plex's catalogue, under Plex's privacy policy.
Overseerr or JellyseerrOnly if you add oneYour requests and searches. It's your own server; artwork for its results comes from TMDB (image.tmdb.org).
LRCLIB (lrclib.net)When you open lyrics and your server has noneThe song's title, artist, album and length.
AniList (anilist.co)When an anime title has no trailer on your serverThe title.
Google PlayOnly if you make a support purchaseThe payment, handled by Google under its own privacy policy. KumaVolt sees the order number, price and country, never your card details.
YouTube or DailymotionOnly when you play a trailerThe trailer request, under their own privacy policies.

These services see your IP address, as with any internet request. None of them receive your files, your library or anything that identifies you to KumaVolt.

Permissions on Android

  • Internet: to reach your servers and the services above.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep music playing with the screen off and show playback controls.

Children

Encheo isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any data from anyone.

This website

encheo.kumavolt.dev sets no cookies and has no analytics. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security under GitHub's privacy statement. To show the newest version on the download links, your browser asks GitHub's API for the latest release. The bar at the top loads from kumavolt.dev and doesn't track you either.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, this page changes with it, with a new date at the top. Questions go in an issue on GitHub.
